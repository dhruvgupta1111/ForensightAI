A CLEAR LINE BETWEEN METHODS

Useful experiments.
Honest boundaries.

The primary assessment and experimental classifier serve different purposes. Their outputs are never presented as equivalent evidence.

PRIMARY SCREENING

Rule-based handling review

The main assessment applies documented rules to the handling conditions entered and explains the resulting indicators and recommendations.

  • Deterministic, explainable factors
  • Recommendations shown with the record
  • Designed as a preliminary review aid
EXPERIMENTAL ONLY

Separate model exploration

Authenticated predictions and experiment comparisons demonstrate a machine-learning workflow using simulated feature profiles and labels.

  • Trained and evaluated on synthetic data
  • Metrics and class-level results available in the workspace
  • Not validated for real-world forensic use
i

Important limitation Neither output confirms contamination. Synthetic training labels are simulated and are not laboratory outcomes or observed forensic evidence.
